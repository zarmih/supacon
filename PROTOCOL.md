# Supacon 7-Day Protocol

Strict version with real-time awareness scoring (1–10).

## Awareness Scale

- **1–3**: Almost full autopilot. You notice actions after they happen.
- **4–6**: Partial presence. Sometimes catch yourself, often automatic.
- **7–8**: Good clarity. See the impulse before or as it arises and can choose.
- **9–10**: High awareness. Clear space between stimulus and response.

**Question**: “How present am I right now, not on autopilot?”

## General Rules (All 7 Days)

- First 15 minutes after waking: no phone.
- Any social media / feed / likes check: 10-second micro-pause + awareness score + “What am I seeking?”
- Max 3 sessions × 15 minutes in social media per day. Timer required.
- One 45-minute single-task focus block (phone in another room).
- Evening written review (5–7 min).

## Daily Structure

### Days 1–2: Calibration
- Micro-pause mandatory, but you may open after scoring.
- Log: time, what you opened, feeling before/after, awareness score.
- Focus block.
- Evening: note 4–6 cases + morning awareness score.

### Days 3–4: Restriction
- 3×15 min limit active.
- After pause + score, only two choices: open within limit or postpone ≥30 min.
- Before posting anything: same pause + score + “Why am I posting this?”
- Focus block continues.

### Days 5–6: Contrast
- Keep limit.
- At least one real-life social contact per day. Compare the feeling to likes.
- Do the focus block at the time you usually feel the strongest urge.
- If you exceed the limit: no self-punishment, just log exactly how much and why + awareness score.

### Day 7: Diagnosis
- Follow rules while observing.
- Evening report:
  - How many micro-pauses worked vs. missed.
  - Strongest 2–3 triggers.
  - Average awareness score and when it was highest/lowest.
  - Did the feeling from likes change?
  - Where the protocol broke most often.

## Micro-Pause Sequence

1. Stop.
2. Score awareness right now (1–10).
3. Answer: “What am I seeking?” (boredom / validation / information / habit / other).
4. Choose: Open now (within limits) / Postpone 30+ min / Close.
5. Optional: second awareness score after the choice.

## Morning Check-in

After 3 breaths: awareness score + name the first desire.

## What You Usually Discover

- Many impulses weaken when named.
- Low awareness (1–4) almost always loses to the impulse.
- High awareness (7+) makes “postpone” natural.
- Real social contact feels different from likes in duration and quality.
