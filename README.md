# Supacon

**A thin awareness layer over ancient instincts in the modern world.**

We are still biologically hunter-gatherers. Dopamine, social validation, and attention systems evolved for scarce resources and small groups. Modern apps exploit them. Supacon is a practical protocol + app concept that inserts a brief metacognitive pause between stimulus and response.

## Core Idea

- Instincts are fuel, not the enemy.
- The goal is interception, not suppression: notice → pause → choose.
- Real-time awareness scoring (1–10) makes the process measurable.

## 7-Day Protocol

See [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) for the full strict version.

### Quick Rules
- No phone for the first 15 minutes after waking.
- Every social media / feed open requires a 10-second micro-pause + awareness score + “What am I seeking?”
- Max 3 × 15 min social media sessions per day.
- One 45-minute single-task focus block daily (phone in another room).
- Evening review with awareness data.

## App Concept

See [APP_SPEC.md](APP_SPEC.md) for screens, flows, and data model.

Key features:
- Full-screen micro-pause overlay with awareness slider.
- Morning check-in.
- Evening review with averages and patterns.
- Dashboard showing awareness trend vs. impulse control.

## Philosophy

The person of the future is not someone who deleted their ancient programs. It is someone who can observe them in real time and choose.
